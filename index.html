A little introduction

Who I am and what I care about

I am a Sanskrit postgraduate currently pursuing my Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) in Sanskrit. My academic journey has been shaped by a deep interest in Sanskrit language, classical literature, Indian knowledge systems and the educational relevance of traditional wisdom.

I completed my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Sanskrit from Chinmaya Vishwa Vidyapeeth. During my academic journey, I received a 100% scholarship and actively participated in academic, cultural and research-oriented activities.

I have gained practical experience through internships, research projects and academic engagements. My internship at Samskriti Foundation, Mysore, provided me with an opportunity to work with traditional Sanskrit texts, particularly Śivatattvaratnākara and Horaśāstra.

I believe Sanskrit is not merely a classical language but a treasure of knowledge, values, culture and intellectual heritage. As an aspiring educator, I wish to make Sanskrit learning meaningful, engaging and accessible to the younger generation.
